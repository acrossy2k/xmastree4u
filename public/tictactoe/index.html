How to Play

  1. Find a friend! This is a local two-player pass-and-play game.
  2. Each player picks their favorite ice cream flavor from a dynamic daily menu.
  3. Take turns placing your custom scoops down on the waffle cone grid.
  4. Connect 3 scoops in a row horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to win!
  5. Once the match concludes, click "Play Again" to pick fresh flavors!
